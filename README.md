## Gul Sher Baloch, PhD

**Applied ML researcher** working on human oversight and reliability of LLM systems.
Associate Professor at Sukkur IBA University · Co-founder & CEO, Leaveit2AI LLC · IEEE Senior Member

My PhD was about recovering clean signals from noisy ones (image denoising and super-resolution). For the last two years I have built LLM systems for teachers and doctors, where a wrong output costs a real person. The question I keep coming back to: **how do humans tell when the model is wrong?**

### Research interests
- Scalable oversight and human review of LLM judgments
- LLM evaluation and reliability in high-stakes domains
- Sycophancy under expert pushback
- Robustness to label noise and distribution shift

### Selected work
| Project | What it is | What it showed |
|---|---|---|
| **GradexAI** | LLM exam grading with a teacher reviewing every grade | 1,946 AI gradings across 17 institutes; teachers approved 91.7%. Open question: how many approvals were wrong? |
| **GulfMedExams** | Medical licensing exam prep for doctors | LLM pipeline checks, tags and deduplicates 6,946 doctor-verified MCQs; an AI resolver closed 93% of 316 error reports. AI kept out of user-facing answers because of hallucination risk. |
| **Label-noise robust deep learning** | IEEE Access, 2025 | Dual selective attention to learn from noisy labels |
